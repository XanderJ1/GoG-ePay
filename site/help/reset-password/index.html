Recover access to your GoG e-payslip account

For an existing account, open the employee application and select “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page. Follow the recovery instructions shown for your account.

Quick answers

What should I do if my payslip verification code does not arrive?

Check the phone number shown by the application and use its available retry option if appropriate. If your recorded number is incorrect or unavailable, contact your employer’s HR or payroll administrator. Do not share an OTP or password with anyone offering help.

How do I recover an existing account?

Recovery screens can change. Use the live application’s instructions rather than assuming that every registration step applies to an existing account.

  1. Start at the official application from this website.
  2. Select “Forgot password?” and supply only the information requested there.
  3. Keep verification codes and security answers private.
  4. After a successful reset, return to sign-in and use the new password.

What if I cannot receive a code?

Check the phone number shown in the application. Use its available retry option if appropriate. If your recorded number is incorrect or unavailable, contact your employer’s HR or payroll administrator for the approved next step. This website cannot change your account details.

Is registration the same as password recovery?

No. The published CAGD first-time registration video also starts at “Forgot password?”. New employees should use the registration guide; returning employees should follow the recovery process presented for their existing account.

What if my employee or Ghana Card details are incorrect?

Check the information you entered against your own employee and Ghana Card records. If the application still reports a mismatch or an employee field is locked, ask HR or payroll to check the record through the approved process. Do not create another account using someone else’s details.
