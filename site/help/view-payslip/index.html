How to check your GoG e-payslip online

Sign in to the employee application, open its payslip area and select the period you want to review. Available options depend on your account.

Quick answers

Can I download my GoG e-payslip?

Use a download or print option if your employee application offers one. Available controls depend on your account and the current application. Keep saved payslips private and remove them from shared devices after use.

What should I check on my payslip?

Check that you are reviewing the correct pay period before comparing amounts.

  1. Confirm the period and employee details.
  2. Review earnings and each deduction.
  3. Check net pay against the figures shown.
  4. Note any missing payslip or unexpected amount for your payroll administrator.

Can I download or print my payslip?

Use a download or print option only if it is available in your application. Available controls depend on your account and the current application. Keep any saved file private and remove it from shared devices after use.

What if a payslip is missing?

Confirm the pay period and record what is missing. Contact your employer’s payroll administrator. Do not send your password or a verification code when asking for help. Sign out when finished.
