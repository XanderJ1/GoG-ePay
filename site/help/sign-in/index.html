How to log in to your GoG e-payslip

To log in to your GoG e-payslip, open gogepayservices.com with your Staff Number and password ready. Enter these details on the employee application’s sign-in screen and follow any verification requested.

Quick answers

How do I log in to the GoG e-payslip application?

Open gogepayservices.com and follow its sign-in screen. Enter your Staff Number and password in the employee application. If you cannot remember your password, use its Forgot password option.

GoG e-payslip password recovery

By GoG ePay Services · Content updated 12 October 2026

Log in to portal

Where do I enter my password?

Enter account details only in the employee application’s authentication flow. This public information website does not collect your password. Start from the official website instead of a copied authentication link.

Why can I not sign in?

Check your Staff Number and password. If you have not set up access, follow the registration guide. If you already have an account but cannot remember its password, use the application’s recovery option. Repeated errors should be referred to the verified support channel or your payroll administrator.

Sign in & find your payslip

STEP 01 / 4

Begin at the right address.

Select “Open the portal” to continue to the existing employee application.

  1. Check the portal address before entering your details.

Good to know You do not enter a password on this information website.

PORTAL ADDRESS gogepayservices.com
Open the portal

STEP 02 / 4

Welcome back.

Enter your Staff Number and Password, then select “Sign In”.

  1. Check your details before submitting.
  2. Follow any additional verification requested by the portal.

Good to know If you need to recover access, use “Forgot password?” and follow the portal’s instructions.

STEP 03 / 4

Give your payslip a moment.

Once signed in, open the payslip area and follow the options available in your account.

  1. Check the pay period.
  2. Review your earnings and deductions.
  3. Check the net pay amount.

Good to know For an unexpected deduction or missing payslip, note the period and contact your payroll administrator.

STEP 04 / 4

All done? Sign out.

Use the portal’s sign-out option when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

  1. Avoid saving your password on public devices.
  2. Keep any downloaded payslip private.
  3. Sign out and close the portal.

Good to know Remove downloaded payslip files from shared devices when you are finished.
