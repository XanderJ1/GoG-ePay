## Browser inspection limits
Chromium visits at 1440 px and 375 px were attempted for both homepages. CHANEL returned Access Denied; Dior returned Page unavailable. Screenshots and title evidence are in reference-captures.

Mobile menu interactions, bag flows, checkout, packaging controls, and detailed footer behaviour could not be verified through these blocked sessions. PENITENT's versions are original demo designs based on the client brief, not claims about those sites' exact implementation.

## PENITENT direction
Original typography pairing, warm paper background, dark olive/ink accents, campaign headline, uncluttered clothing cards, clear variant controls, and an editorial story panel. Product and checkout screens prioritise readable information and explicit demo feedback. No CHANEL/DIOR logos, images, or copy are used.

## Placeholder assets
Clothing/campaign photographs are hotlinked from images.unsplash.com. Unsplash's standard licence allows commercial and non-commercial image use: https://unsplash.com/license. The seed file lists the exact image identifiers; the campaign source is photo-1483985988355-763728e1935b. Photographs are illustrative rather than exact garment/colour representations, and are labelled as such on product pages. Final brand photography and product images must replace them before real sales.

DM Sans and Manrope are loaded through Google Fonts with system-font fallbacks. If external asset hosts are unavailable, shopping logic remains database-backed; final production work can self-host approved photography and font files.
